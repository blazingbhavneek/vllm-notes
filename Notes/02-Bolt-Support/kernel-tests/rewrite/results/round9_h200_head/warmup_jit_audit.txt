Inference-time JIT
------------------

  during inference: _causal_conv1d_fwd_kernel. This causes a latency spike;
  consider extending warmup to cover this shape/config.

This is the Mamba causal-conv1d kernel, not a ModelOpt block-quant kernel.
It fired once, during the first c=8 run, and did not repeat for any later pass
or for GSM8K. It is the pre-existing Mamba warmup gap (the log also reports a
missing selective_state_update config for dstate=128 on this H200 NVL), not a
regression from PR #65003.
